New
- Continue reading right from a series page on iPad, Mac, and Apple TV with the restored inline resume button.

Improvements
- Password logins now automatically upgrade to app-specific API keys, making background downloads and syncing more reliable.
- Collections and read lists now load in pages as you scroll, and tags, authors, publishers, and genres sort in your locale's order.
- Reading progress updates faster when you return to the app, and offline progress is always pushed to the server before pulling.
- Handoff, reading Live Activities, and keep-screen-awake settings now live together in a new System Features page.

Fixes
- Fixed dual-page Page Curl freezing after the first turn in landscape.
- Fixed a Mac and iPad crash at launch when collections or read lists existed.
- Hardened downloads against unauthorized or corrupt files; broken EPUB downloads are now detected and re-downloaded.
- Fixed PDF single-tap navigation, controls hiding while a menu is open, and read list/collection pickers freezing on macOS.
- Collections and read lists appear reliably on series and book pages again, and lists keep their scroll position when refreshing after reading.
- EPUB open failures now report the actual cause instead of a generic error.
- Fixed dashboard cards looking darker at the trailing edge.
